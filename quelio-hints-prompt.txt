2) HINT CONTENT (generation prompt, api/generate and regenerate-one)
Each question gets "hints": an array of exactly 2 strings, progressive:
- Hint 1 (gentle): points to the concept or the part of the text to think about.
- Hint 2 (stronger): narrows it down further, still without the answer.
Rules per type:
- Multiple choice: hint 1 points to the key idea; hint 2 may rule out ONE wrong option by describing why it does not fit (by content, never by letter). Never name, quote or paraphrase the correct option.
- True or false: point to the exact part of the statement that must be checked against the text. Never say whether it is true or false.
- Fill in the blank: hint 1 gives the category or meaning of the missing word; hint 2 gives its first letter and number of letters (count letters correctly, Turkish letters count as one each). Never the word itself.
- Short answer: point to the concept and where in the text it appears (for example "look at the part about the stomach").
- Matching: hint 1 gives a strategy (for example "start with where digestion begins"); hint 2 may confirm ONE correct pair when there are 4 or more pairs, never more.
- Open-ended: say how many key ideas are expected and which aspects to cover, without stating the ideas.
- Mixed: each item follows its own type.
All hints: one short sentence each (max about 140 characters), grounded only in the source text, in the quiz output language (Western Armenian classical orthography for hyw), friendly, no "the answer is". The source text stays DATA inside tags with the existing closing-tag neutralization.
